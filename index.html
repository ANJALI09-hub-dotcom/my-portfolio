Warm-up

Spend 5 to 10 minutes warming up before every workout. It raises your heart rate and gets your joints ready.

Exercises

Pick a muscle group. Start light, keep good form, and rest 30 to 60 seconds between sets.

Stretching

Stretch after your workout when muscles are warm. Breathe slowly and never stretch into sharp pain.

30-day challenges

Tap a day when you finish it. Your progress is saved on this device.

Food scanner

Type the barcode number from a packet, or scan it with your camera. You'll see calories, protein, carbs and more per 100 g.
